## Other projects

**Aerial reconstruction and 3D Gaussian Splatting.** I captured multi-view aerial imagery, reproduced a reconstruction pipeline, and explored trade-offs among visual detail, rendering speed and memory use.

Basic demo to add: a short walkthrough of image capture, reconstruction and a matched-view output comparison. Any new reproduction will be identified separately from the original high-school work.

**Physical controls for mobile photography.** I iterated mechanical and Hall-sensor/Bluetooth HID camera-control prototypes, then explored capacitive input and haptic feedback.

Basic demo to add: annotated prototype images and an input-to-camera walkthrough, showing which functions worked and which remained exploratory.

**Interactive Prisoner's Dilemma exhibit.** I built a QR-accessible strategy game and designed exhibition visuals for Duke Math Meet China 2024; our team received the Best Visual Design Prize.

Basic demo to add: a strategy-choice walkthrough with the exhibition context. A rebuilt version will be labeled as a reconstruction, and the award remains attributed to the team.

**Cloud Photograph.** I contributed to the design and deployment of a photography community website, alongside community organization and communication.

Basic demo to add: a short gallery-browsing walkthrough on desktop and a narrow screen, using images cleared for public presentation.

**Photography and audiovisual work.** I produced school and event media, edited images and sound, and contributed to the film *Duck Blood and Vermicelli Soup*.

Basic demo to add: a small selection of permitted stills or short excerpts, with my role identified for each piece.
