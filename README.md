# Jinrui Li

**Memory · Perception · Interaction**

I build tools for learning and human–AI collaboration, drawing on work in photography, sound, and physical interfaces. I'm interested in how intelligent systems maintain context and revise their internal models as new evidence arrives.

Electrical Engineering at UC Irvine · [Email](mailto:jinruil4@uci.edu)

## Current work

### Raymond Layer
**Persistent memory and context for AI assistants** · Personal deployment

Long-running work needs continuity: completed tasks should stay completed, decisions should retain their reasoning, and claims should remain tied to evidence. I designed a persistent context workflow around these requirements, with explicit authorization, revision checks, and recovery rules for updates across sessions.

### Oasis
**A desktop workspace for learning** · Prototype in development

Built around the course, from source material to bilingual lecture records, notes, and review. I define the product workflows and interaction design, including a learning-state model that keeps self-assessment, original answers, and revealed solutions separate.

## Selected work

- **Aerial reconstruction & 3D Gaussian Splatting** — Captured multi-view aerial imagery and reproduced the reconstruction pipeline. Explored detail, rendering speed, and memory trade-offs through monitoring and Gaussian-pruning scripts.
- **Physical camera controls** — Iterated from a mechanical dial to Hall sensing and Bluetooth HID, then explored capacitive input and haptic feedback. The work connects camera handling, sensing, and tactile interaction.
- **Interactive Prisoner's Dilemma exhibit** — Designed and wrote the main code for a QR-accessible strategy game, with posters and slides for Duke Math Meet China 2024. Team award: **Best Visual Design Prize**.

Photography, audiovisual production, and piano are part of how I think about tools and attention. I've contributed to event media and film, helped build and operate a photography community of over 1,000 members, contributed to its website, and founded a school photography club.
